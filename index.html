About us

A farm business started from scratch, on land with room to grow.

We are at the beginning, and we say so plainly. What we offer partners is a clear place, a clear purpose and the chance to shape the enterprise from its first season.

Libala Gold Agribusiness Limited is a Zambian start-up agricultural company based in Sesheke District, in the country's Western Province. Our land lies on the plains beside the Zambezi, one of Africa's great rivers.

Our name and our emblem tell the story of what we intend to build: livestock and grain, raised on the Sesheke plains and sold well beyond them.

We are establishing the company step by step, with proper structure and records from day one, so that investors and partners can see exactly where the business stands at each stage.

Our focus

What Libala Gold is being built around

Cattle

Livestock is at the heart of our emblem and of farming life in Western Province. Beef cattle are a core part of our plan.

Maize

Maize is Zambia's staple grain. Crop production sits alongside livestock so that each side of the farm supports the other.

The Zambezi

Our location beside the river shapes everything we plan to do. Water is the asset that makes the Sesheke plains worth farming.

To the nations

Sesheke is a border town on a regional trade route. Our ambition is to supply local markets first and regional markets in time.

Location

On the Zambezi, at a regional crossroads

Sesheke stands on the north bank of the Zambezi in south-western Zambia. A road bridge links the town to Katima Mulilo in Namibia, and the M10 road carries traffic east to Livingstone and on to the rest of the country.

  • DistrictSesheke, Western Province, Zambia
  • RiverZambezi, north bank
  • BorderKatima Mulilo Bridge to Namibia
  • RoadM10, part of the Walvis Bay–Ndola–Lubumbashi corridor
  • Nearest cityLivingstone and Victoria Falls, about 200 km east
